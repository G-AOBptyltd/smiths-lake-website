About this survey

MidCoast Council has released two concept designs for Blueys Beach Village. Most features are identical across both options — only three features genuinely differ. This survey focuses on those differences and your support for the shared elements.

Your response is anonymous and will contribute to the PPCA's community submission to MidCoast Council. Completing both this PPCA survey and the Council's own Have Your Say form maximises your impact.

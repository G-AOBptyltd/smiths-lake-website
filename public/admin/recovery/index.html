A recovery event is one fire, flood or storm. Open it on Standby before an event or as it unfolds, move it to Active recovery while the work is happening, Monitoring as things wind down, then Closed. Inside an event you keep the needs register (who needs what) and the offers of help, match one to the other, and export the whole thing as evidence for a recovery-funding claim.

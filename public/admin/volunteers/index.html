Help

The 60-second guide.

How volunteers arrive

Every group page on the website (Environment, Groups & Activities, Emergency) has a "Volunteer for this group" form. Signups land in the Roster as Applied — the group's steward (and the village notify list) gets an email. Approve them to make them Active.

Stewards

A community steward runs one or more cards — they see only their groups' volunteers and activities in this portal. Village admins appoint stewards from the Stewards tab; the invitation and access are wired automatically.

Recording hours

Create an activity (a working bee, a planting day, a training session), pick the group, add who came and their hours. Save keeps it as a Draft; press Confirm when the numbers are right — confirmed hours are what the village can later claim as in-kind co-contribution on grants.

The money link

In the next stage, a village admin will review confirmed activities and push them into the Contributions portal as time-in-kind entries — every claimed dollar traceable back to a named session and its hours. Until then, everything you record here is already building that audit trail.

Removing people

Use Inactive for volunteers who've moved on. Full deletion is reserved for the super-admin.

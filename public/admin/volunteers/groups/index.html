Sign-up groups

Choose which of your website's groups and activities appear in the Village1st Volunteer sign-up app. Turn one on and residents can sign up to it; turn it off and it disappears from the app. Whether it's a volunteer group, interest group or club — and whether it asks the health & safety questions — is set automatically from the card's category, so you only choose which ones to offer. Info and reference cards aren't listed.

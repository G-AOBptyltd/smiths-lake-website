Help

The 60-second guide.

Running an event

Press New event, fill in the details (capacity blank = unlimited) and Save — it starts as a Draft nobody can see. Press Publish when you're ready and it appears on villagefirst.org.au/events instantly.

Registrations

People register on the website (parties up to 10). They get a confirmation email automatically; when the event is full, new registrations go to the waitlist and are told so honestly. Promote waitlisted parties from the door list when places free up — the tool re-checks capacity for you.

The night of

Open the event on your phone. The door list shows every party — tick Arrived as they come in, and record door takings against each party if the event is paid. ⬇ Door list CSV prints nicely if you'd rather run paper.

Money

v1 is pay-at-the-door — record takings per party so the night reconciles. Online ticket sales switch on with each village's own Tyro merchant later; nothing here changes.

Cancelling

Close stops new registrations but keeps the event visible in your list; Cancel pulls it from the website. Email the attendees from your usual email using the CSV — automatic cancellation emails are a later stage.

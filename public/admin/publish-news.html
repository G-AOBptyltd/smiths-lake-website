Publish News to the Website

Finished writing or updating a news story in Notion? Use this button to put it live.

  1. In Notion, open the 📰 News Desk view
  2. Make sure your story has Status on Web = Published, Show in News Feed ✓, and a Publish Date
  3. Click the button below — the website updates in about 3 minutes

This rebuilds villagefirst.org.au with the latest Notion content. It's safe to click — nothing is deleted.

📰 News Desk Playbook

For anyone who keeps the Village News page alive. Console: Admin → News desk

Publish a news story (the everyday job)

  1. Open Write a story. Give it a headline, type your story (blank line = new paragraph), check the date.
  2. Press Save & put in feed.
  3. Go to Stories and press Publish to website. The site updates in about 3 minutes.

Share something already on the website

  1. Open Share existing. Filter by section if it helps (History, Services, Groups, Environment, Projects, Emergency).
  2. Click the item, press Add to news feed, then Publish. It joins the feed with today's date and links to its existing page — nothing is retyped or duplicated.

Take a story down (Archive)

  • On Stories, press Archive on the story, then Publish. It comes off the website and the feed completely, but stays saved — the card shows an Archived chip.
  • Changed your mind? Press Restore on the archived story, then Publish, and it's back.
  • Just want it out of the feed but keep the page live? Use the In news feed toggle instead of Archive.

Deleting a story

  • Only a Super Village Admin can delete. Press Delete on a story, confirm, then Publish — it's removed from the website and tidied away in Notion (recoverable from Notion's trash if needed).
  • If you're a regular admin you'll see Request delete instead. Press it and the Super Village Admin gets a "🗑 delete requested" flag on that story to action. (Not sure you need it gone? Archive is the safe, reversible choice.)

Drafts

  • Save as draft keeps a story completely off the website until you're ready. Drafts show an amber chip on the Stories page.

Photos

  • Add a photo right in Write a story: click Photo, choose a picture from your computer, and you'll see a preview. It uploads when you press Save — no Notion needed. Large photos are shrunk automatically.
  • To change a photo later, open the story in Stories → edit, and pick a new file. To swap it for none, that's still done in Notion for now.
  • Stories with a photo get the big picture cards; stories without get a smart coloured panel.
💡 The feed shows newest Publish Date first. Editing an old page never bumps it into the news — only the date and the "In news feed" toggle decide what appears.

🤝 Contributions Playbook

Keep a tidy record of everything the community gives, and celebrate supporters. Console: Admin → Contributions

What this is for

One ledger for every kind of support: money, payments, gifts, time in kind (volunteer hours) and donated services. Residents can also offer support themselves on the public Support Us page.

Log a contribution (the everyday job)

  1. Open Log a contribution. Pick the kind (money, gift, time in kind, donated service…).
  2. Type who gave it, then the amount (dollars) — or hours for time in kind.
  3. Set the status: Received (it's in) or Pledged (promised, not yet arrived). Add a note if useful.
  4. Press Save contribution. It appears instantly in the Ledger — no publishing step.

Pledges from the website

  • When a resident fills in the Pledge or offer form on /contribute, it lands in your Ledger automatically as Pledged, marked "by public form".
  • Follow up using the contact they left, and when it arrives, open the row and change the status to Received.

Fixing a mistake — Archive or Delete

  • Every row in the Ledger now has Archive (or Restore, once archived) and, for admins, Delete — the same pattern as News Desk's Archive/Delete.
  • Archive is the safe, reversible choice: the entry stays in Notion and in the Ledger (shown greyed-out with an Archived chip), but drops out of the totals at the top. Press Restore anytime to bring it back — it returns as Received, so re-check the status if it should be Pledged.
  • Delete is for genuine mistakes (duplicate entries, test data). It's available to a Village Admin or Super Village Admin — Survey Stewards don't see the Delete button. Deleted entries move to Notion's trash (recoverable there if needed) and disappear from the Ledger immediately.
  • There's no publishing step — Archive, Restore and Delete all take effect instantly, same as logging a contribution.

The public Supporters board — opt-in only

  • The fun leaderboard lives at villagefirst.org.au/supporters, with a card and shout-outs on the home page.
  • Nobody is named without permission. A supporter only appears if they tick "thank me publicly" on the form — or, with their say-so, you tick Show publicly on their entry.
  • Add a Display name for how they should appear (e.g. "Jane S." or "Smiths Lake Bakery"). Entries on the board show a 🎉 public badge in the Ledger.
  • The board shows playful tiers (Lake Legend, Champion, Mate, Supporter) and never shows dollar amounts. Everyone else is still counted, anonymously, in the totals.

Taking card donations (money)

  • Online memberships and card donations will run through Tyro on PPCA's own merchant account, and switch on once Tyro enables online (eCommerce) payments. Until then the pledge form is the live way to record support, and the payments card shows "coming soon".
💡 Every pledge is saved to the Ledger. An optional email to PPCA on each new pledge can be switched on later — until then, check the Ledger to see what's come in.

🪪 Membership Playbook

The PPCA member register — from application to financial member. Console: Admin → Membership (Village Admins only)

How members arrive

Residents apply on villagefirst.org.au/membership. The application lands in the Register instantly with an amber Applied chip, and the committee gets a notification email. Nothing to publish, nothing to set up.

The member lifecycle (the everyday job)

  1. Applied — a new application. Press Approve (on the row, or inside the member) once the committee is happy.
  2. Approved — a member awaiting payment. Press Send welcome email — it confirms the approval and explains how to pay.
  3. Paid — press Record payment when the fee arrives: date, method, a reference (bank transfer description or receipt number) and the amount. That makes them financial, and the "Fees collected" total ticks up.
  4. Lapsed — for members who don't renew or have left. Nothing is deleted; the history stays.

Renewals (each new year from 1 July)

  • The membership year runs 1 July – 30 June. Each year is its own row in the register, so past years stay untouched — that's your audit trail for AGMs.
  • Open the Renewals tab: it lists every member from earlier years with no row for the new year. Press Create renewal row (starts them as Approved, unpaid) and/or Send reminder.
  • When their fee arrives, record the payment on the new row as usual.

The AGM / committee export

  • Filter the register to what you need — e.g. Paid + the current year — and press ⬇ CSV. The export matches exactly what's on screen, ready for the AGM or the auditor.

Fixing things

  • Open any member to correct names, addresses, contact details or the membership type — every change records who made it and when.
  • Deleting a register row entirely (typos, duplicates, test entries) is reserved for the Super Village Admin. For real members who've left, use Lapsed.
💡 Member details are personal information — that's why the Membership console is Village Admin only, and why exports should only go to committee members who need them.

🙋 Volunteer Hub Playbook

Group rosters, working bees and the hours behind grant applications. Group leaders get a simple phone-friendly home at /admin/volunteers/my/ (they land there automatically); Village Admins also have the full console.

The one idea to understand

Every community group has a page ("card") on the website — Landcare, the fire shed, a social group. The Volunteer hub organises everything per card: who volunteers for it, what activities it ran, and how many hours went in. A Community Steward is a volunteer trusted to run one or more cards — they sign in to this same admin portal and see only their groups.

How volunteers arrive

  • Group pages on the website (Environment, Groups & Activities, Emergency sections) carry a "Volunteer for this group" form. Signups land in the Roster as Applied, and the group's steward gets an email.
  • Press Approve to make them Active. Someone volunteering for a second group is recognised by their email — the new group is simply added to their record.

Recording a working bee (the everyday job)

  1. Open Activities → New activity. Name it ("March working bee — foreshore weeding"), pick the group and date.
  2. Add who came and their hours — start typing a name and roster members are suggested; people not on the roster can be typed in free.
  3. Save keeps it as a Draft. Press Confirm when the numbers are right.

Why the hours matter (grants)

  • Confirmed hours are the village's in-kind co-contribution evidence for grant applications — every claimed hour traceable to a named session, a date and a group.
  • In the next stage, a Village Admin will push confirmed activities into the Contributions portal as time-in-kind entries; a pushed activity locks so the financial record can't drift.

Appointing a Community Steward (Village Admins)

  1. Open the Stewards tab, enter their email, tick the card(s) they'll run, press Appoint steward.
  2. If they don't have a sign-in yet, an invitation email goes out automatically and their access is ready the moment they accept. No Netlify, no technical steps.
  3. To change their groups later: Edit cards. To take access away: Remove — they keep their sign-in but see no groups.
💡 Stewards can do everything in this playbook for their own cards — approve volunteers, record activities, confirm hours. They can't see other groups, membership records or village-wide admin.

🏛️ Facility Bookings Playbook

The hall booking book. Console: Admin → Facility bookings (Village Admins). Public page: /facilities/

How requests arrive

Residents see the rates and a two-month availability calendar on villagefirst.org.au/facilities and send a request. It lands in the Booking book as Requested and the committee gets an email — with a ⚠️ flag if the times overlap something already in the book. Nothing is ever auto-confirmed.

Confirming a booking (the everyday job)

  1. Open the request. Check the ⚠️ clash flag and the purpose.
  2. Set the fee quoted if it differs from the standard rates (the bond pre-fills from the facility).
  3. Press Confirm booking, then Send confirmation email — it carries the fee, bond, bank details and the conditions of hire.

Money & the bond

  • When the transfer arrives, record it under Fee, bond & payment — date, amount, bank reference.
  • After the event and the hall inspection, tick Bond returned. The yearly "hire fees received" total sits at the top of the book.
  • Online card payment switches on with Tyro later — this book doesn't change.

Declining, cancelling, completing

  • Decline requests that don't work and send the decline email — it invites them to reply and find another time.
  • Cancelled is for confirmed bookings that fall through; Completed once keys are back and the hall's checked. Nothing is deleted — the book is the record.

Rates & conditions

Edit them under Facilities & rates — the public page updates instantly, no publish step. The conditions text also rides along in every confirmation email.

🎟️ Events Playbook

One-off community events — trivia nights, markets, meetings. Console: Admin → Events. Public page: /events/

Creating an event

  1. New event → name, description, date and time, location, capacity (leave blank for unlimited) and the price as plain words ("$15, kids free") — money is collected at the door for now.
  2. Save keeps it as a Draft nobody can see. Publish puts it on villagefirst.org.au/events instantly — no publish-website step needed.

Registrations, capacity & the waitlist

  • People register on the website in parties of up to 10 and get an automatic confirmation email. The committee gets a heads-up email per registration.
  • When the event fills, new registrations go to the waitlist — and they're told so honestly on the spot. If places free up, press Promote on a waitlisted party; the tool re-checks capacity so you can't accidentally oversell.

Running the door

  • Open the event on your phone on the night. Tick ✓ Arrived as parties come in, and press $ door to record what each party paid — the night reconciles itself.
  • Prefer paper? ⬇ Door list CSV gives you a printable list.

Closing, cancelling, finishing

  • Close registrations stops new signups but keeps the event listed. Cancel pulls it from the website — download the CSV first and email attendees from your usual email.
  • After the night, Mark completed. Door takings show in the console's yearly total.
💡 Online ticket sales (card payment at registration) switch on when the village's own Tyro merchant goes live — the events book stays exactly the same.

🌐 Publishing Playbook

How villagefirst.org.au actually updates. Console: Admin → Publish

The one idea to understand

The website is rebuilt from Notion content each time someone presses Publish. Changing things in Notion or the News Desk does not change the live site by itself — pressing Publish does.

When to press it

  • After saving or toggling news stories in the News Desk.
  • After editing website content in Notion (services, groups, history, projects…).

Good to know

  • It's safe — nothing is ever deleted by publishing. It just refreshes the site with the latest content.
  • Updates take about 3 minutes to appear. There's no need to press it twice.
  • The Publish buttons in the News Desk and on the Publish page do the same thing.

👥 Roles & Access Playbook

Who can do what, and how to invite someone. Console: Surveys → Access

The roles

  • Super Admin — everything, every village (Greg / AOB).
  • Village Admin — full control of their village: surveys, news desk, publishing, settings, access.
  • Survey Steward — runs surveys and the News Desk for their village. The right role for most volunteers.
  • Community Steward — a steward whose home is the Volunteer hub: they run the roster, activities and hours for the specific group card(s) a Village Admin assigns them. Appointed from the Volunteer hub's Stewards tab (not the Surveys console) — the invite and access are wired automatically.
  • Committee Viewer — read-only: can see results, can't change anything.

To give someone news publishing (e.g. Rob)

  1. If they're already a Village Admin or Survey Steward — they already have it. Just send them the bookmark: villagefirst.org.au/admin/
  2. For someone new: open Surveys → Access, enter their email, choose Survey Steward (or Village Admin), press Send invite.
  3. They accept the emailed invite, set a password, and sign in at /admin/. Roles take effect on their next sign-in.
💡 One login works across every console — Surveys, News Desk, Publish — and the village picker follows them between consoles.

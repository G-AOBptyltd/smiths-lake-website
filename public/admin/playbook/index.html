📰 News Desk Playbook

For anyone who keeps the Village News page alive. Console: Admin → News desk

Publish a news story (the everyday job)

  1. Open Write a story. Give it a headline, type your story (blank line = new paragraph), check the date.
  2. Press Save & put in feed.
  3. Go to Stories and press Publish to website. The site updates in about 3 minutes.

Share something already on the website

  1. Open Share existing. Filter by section if it helps (History, Services, Groups, Environment, Projects, Emergency).
  2. Click the item, press Add to news feed, then Publish. It joins the feed with today's date and links to its existing page — nothing is retyped or duplicated.

Take a story down (Archive)

  • On Stories, press Archive on the story, then Publish. It comes off the website and the feed completely, but stays saved — the card shows an Archived chip.
  • Changed your mind? Press Restore on the archived story, then Publish, and it's back.
  • Just want it out of the feed but keep the page live? Use the In news feed toggle instead of Archive.

Deleting a story

  • Only a Super Village Admin can delete. Press Delete on a story, confirm, then Publish — it's removed from the website and tidied away in Notion (recoverable from Notion's trash if needed).
  • If you're a regular admin you'll see Request delete instead. Press it and the Super Village Admin gets a "🗑 delete requested" flag on that story to action. (Not sure you need it gone? Archive is the safe, reversible choice.)

Drafts

  • Save as draft keeps a story completely off the website until you're ready. Drafts show an amber chip on the Stories page.

Photos

  • Add a photo right in Write a story: click Photo, choose a picture from your computer, and you'll see a preview. It uploads when you press Save — no Notion needed. Large photos are shrunk automatically.
  • To change a photo later, open the story in Stories → edit, and pick a new file. To swap it for none, that's still done in Notion for now.
  • Stories with a photo get the big picture cards; stories without get a smart coloured panel.
💡 The feed shows newest Publish Date first. Editing an old page never bumps it into the news — only the date and the "In news feed" toggle decide what appears.

🤝 Contributions Playbook

Keep a tidy record of everything the community gives, and celebrate supporters. Console: Admin → Contributions

What this is for

One ledger for every kind of support: money, payments, gifts, time in kind (volunteer hours) and donated services. Residents can also offer support themselves on the public Support Us page.

Log a contribution (the everyday job)

  1. Open Log a contribution. Pick the kind (money, gift, time in kind, donated service…).
  2. Type who gave it, then the amount (dollars) — or hours for time in kind.
  3. Set the status: Received (it's in) or Pledged (promised, not yet arrived). Add a note if useful.
  4. Press Save contribution. It appears instantly in the Ledger — no publishing step.

Pledges from the website

  • When a resident fills in the Pledge or offer form on /contribute, it lands in your Ledger automatically as Pledged, marked "by public form".
  • Follow up using the contact they left, and when it arrives, open the row and change the status to Received.

The public Supporters board — opt-in only

  • The fun leaderboard lives at villagefirst.org.au/supporters, with a card and shout-outs on the home page.
  • Nobody is named without permission. A supporter only appears if they tick "thank me publicly" on the form — or, with their say-so, you tick Show publicly on their entry.
  • Add a Display name for how they should appear (e.g. "Jane S." or "Smiths Lake Bakery"). Entries on the board show a 🎉 public badge in the Ledger.
  • The board shows playful tiers (Lake Legend, Champion, Mate, Supporter) and never shows dollar amounts. Everyone else is still counted, anonymously, in the totals.

Taking card donations (money)

  • Online card donations run through Stripe and switch on once PPCA provides a bank account for its own VillageFirst Stripe account. Until then the pledge form is the live way to record support, and the Donate buttons show "coming soon".
💡 Every pledge is saved to the Ledger. An optional email to PPCA on each new pledge can be switched on later — until then, check the Ledger to see what's come in.

🌐 Publishing Playbook

How villagefirst.org.au actually updates. Console: Admin → Publish

The one idea to understand

The website is rebuilt from Notion content each time someone presses Publish. Changing things in Notion or the News Desk does not change the live site by itself — pressing Publish does.

When to press it

  • After saving or toggling news stories in the News Desk.
  • After editing website content in Notion (services, groups, history, projects…).

Good to know

  • It's safe — nothing is ever deleted by publishing. It just refreshes the site with the latest content.
  • Updates take about 3 minutes to appear. There's no need to press it twice.
  • The Publish buttons in the News Desk and on the Publish page do the same thing.

👥 Roles & Access Playbook

Who can do what, and how to invite someone. Console: Surveys → Access

The roles

  • Super Admin — everything, every village (Greg / AOB).
  • Village Admin — full control of their village: surveys, news desk, publishing, settings, access.
  • Survey Steward — runs surveys and the News Desk for their village. The right role for most volunteers.
  • Committee Viewer — read-only: can see results, can't change anything.

To give someone news publishing (e.g. Rob)

  1. If they're already a Village Admin or Survey Steward — they already have it. Just send them the bookmark: villagefirst.org.au/admin/
  2. For someone new: open Surveys → Access, enter their email, choose Survey Steward (or Village Admin), press Send invite.
  3. They accept the emailed invite, set a password, and sign in at /admin/. Roles take effect on their next sign-in.
💡 One login works across every console — Surveys, News Desk, Publish — and the village picker follows them between consoles.

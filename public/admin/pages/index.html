Help

The 60-second guide.

What these pages are

The About Us page on your website is built from the story rows listed here — your village's "our story" page plus companions like the Indigenous heritage page, in their listed order. The Contact page comes from the contact row. Edit them here and the website follows.

To change a page

1. Click Edit on the page. 2. Change the title, summary, or full body text. 3. Save changes. 4. When you're done, press Publish on the Publish website tile — the site updates about 3 minutes later.

Summary vs page body

If a page has a body, the website shows the body; otherwise it shows the summary. Pages laid out with photos can't be edited as plain text here — edit those in Notion or via the News Desk.

Something's marked "Hidden"?

Hidden pages exist but don't render on the website (they're not set to Published/Show on Website in Notion). Ask your super admin if one should be live.

Help

The 60-second guide.

Selling a placement

Agree the deal with the business (tier, dates, fee), then press New advertiser and record it. It starts as a Draft; press Activate once they're confirmed — placements only show publicly while Active and inside their dates.

Tiers

Major Sponsor tops the strip with a highlight; Sponsor and Supporter follow. Tiers (and what they cost) are whatever the committee decides — the tool just displays them in order.

Money

Record the fee against the placement when the transfer lands (date, amount, reference) — the yearly total sits at the top. Card payment arrives with the village's own Tyro merchant later.

When it ends

Placements past their end date drop off the public strip automatically. Mark them Expired to tidy the list, or extend the dates and keep them Active for a renewal.

Where do the ads appear?

The Public preview tab shows the strip exactly as it will render. It is not on the website yet — placement locations are a committee decision, and switching a location on is a one-line change once decided.

Help

The 60-second guide.

Updating a listing (the everyday job)

Click the service, fix the description, hours, address or contact details, and press Save & publish website. The live site updates in about 3 minutes. Plain Save stores your changes without touching the website — good for drafting.

Service Stewards

Village admins can hand each listing to the person who actually runs the service — the bowlo manager updates the bowlo. Appoint them under Service stewards: they sign in and see only their listings. Invitations and access are automatic.

What stewards can't do

Renaming a service or adding/removing listings stays with the village admins (a rename changes the page's web address). Contact the committee for those.

Contact privacy

Untick "Show these contact details publicly" to keep the contact person/email/phone out of the public page while still having them on record for the committee.

Help

The 60-second guide.

How bookings arrive

Residents request the hall on villagefirst.org.au/facilities — they see the rates and a calendar of taken days first. Requests land here as Requested and the committee gets an email (with a ⚠️ flag if it clashes with an existing booking).

Confirming (the everyday job)

Open the request, check the calendar didn't double-book, set the fee quoted if it differs from the standard rates, press Confirm, then Send confirmation email — it carries the fee, bond, payment details and the conditions of hire.

Money

When the transfer lands, record it under Fee, bond & payment (date, amount, reference). After the event and inspection, tick Bond returned. Online card payment arrives with Tyro later — nothing changes in this book when it does.

Declining & cancelling

Decline a request that doesn't work (send the decline email — it invites them to pick another time). Cancelled is for confirmed bookings that fall through. Nothing is ever deleted — the book is the record.

After the event

Mark it Completed once the keys are back and the hall's checked. That frees the record for the bond return tick.

Rates

Edit rates and conditions under Facilities & rates — the public page updates immediately.

Help

The 60-second guide.

The member lifecycle

Applied (came in from the website form) → Approve at a committee meeting → Record payment when the fee arrives (that makes them financial) → Lapsed if a year ends unrenewed. Each membership year is its own row, so the register doubles as a year-by-year audit trail.

Recording a payment

Open the member, press Record payment, check the date/amount and add a reference (bank transfer description or receipt number). Status flips to Paid automatically.

Renewals

When a new membership year starts (1 July), the Renewals tab lists everyone who hasn't renewed. Create renewal row starts their new year (Approved, unpaid) and Send reminder emails them. Members who never respond just stay lapsed — nothing to clean up.

Emails

Welcome confirms an approval (and explains how to pay if they haven't); Renewal reminder nudges for the new year. Every send is stamped on the member's row so you can see what went when.

Exporting for the AGM

Filter the register (e.g. Paid + the current year) and press ⬇ CSV — the export matches exactly what's on screen.

Removing a row

Use Lapsed for members who've left. Deleting a register row entirely is reserved for the super-admin (typos, duplicates, test entries).

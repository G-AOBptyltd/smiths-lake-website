Help

The 60-second guide.

To publish a news story

1. Click Write a story — headline, story, date. 2. Save & put in feed. 3. Back on Stories, press Publish to website. Done — live in ~3 minutes.

To share something already on the website

Use Share existing, click the item (a project, a group, a history page…), confirm, publish. It appears in the feed with today's date and links to its page.

To take a story down

On Stories, switch its toggle off, then press Publish.

Photos

Add photos in Notion (Hero Image File on the story). Ask Greg if you'd like this set up for you.

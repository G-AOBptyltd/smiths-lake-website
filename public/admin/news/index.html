Help

The 60-second guide.

To publish a news story

1. Click Write a story and pick a layout — three are built for lots of photos (Photo Story, Event Recap & Gallery, Photo Essay); two are quick with a photo or two (Announcement, Simple Update). 2. Fill in the headline, a one-line summary, and each section — add photos where you like. 3. Save & put in feed, then on Stories press Publish to website. Live in ~3 minutes.

To share something already on the website

Use Share existing, click the item (a project, a group, a history page…), confirm, publish. It appears in the feed with today's date and links to its page.

To take a story down

On Stories, press Archive, then Publish — it leaves the site and feed but stays saved (press Restore to bring it back). To only drop it from the feed but keep its page, use the toggle instead.

To delete a story

Only a Super Village Admin sees Delete. Regular admins press Request delete and the Super Admin gets a flag to action it. Deleted stories go to Notion's trash (recoverable). When unsure, Archive is the safe, reversible choice.

Photos

In Write a story, click Photo and choose a picture from your computer — it uploads when you Save. No Notion needed. Stories with a photo get the big picture card; without, a smart coloured panel.

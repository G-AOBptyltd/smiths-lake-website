Help

The 60-second guide.

Logging a contribution

Pick the kind (money, payment, gift, time in kind, or a donated service), type who gave it, add the amount or hours, and press Save contribution. It's stored instantly — no publishing step.

Amount vs Hours

For money, payments, gifts and donated services, use Amount (a dollar value — for gifts/services put your best estimate, or leave blank). For Time in kind, use Hours instead.

Received vs Pledged

Set Pledged when someone has promised a contribution that hasn't arrived yet — pledged money is totalled separately in the Ledger. Change it to Received once it's in.

Fixing a mistake

Open the Ledger, click the row, change what you need and Save.

Taking real card payments

This portal records contributions. Accepting card donations online (via Stripe) is the next step — ask your VillageFirst admin to switch it on.

Pop-up store
Seasons and the seller register
How the money works. Every seller is paid directly by the buyer, into the seller's own account, using the seller's own payment link or arrangement. The village never holds, pools or forwards a seller's money — so these takings are not village income, and the committee is not acting as a payment service. One order always belongs to one seller, which is what keeps settlement and refunds clean.
Loading…
A season is one pop-up: a summer market, a Christmas fundraiser, a one-weekend art trail. Create it as a Draft, add sellers and their range, set it Open when trading starts, and Closed when the season ends. Nothing about a closed season disappears — the orders stay as a trading record.
A seller goes Live only once the committee has recorded how that seller gets paid directly and ticked the confirmation. Until then their products cannot go Active and no orders can be taken — that is the safeguard that keeps the village out of other people's money.
